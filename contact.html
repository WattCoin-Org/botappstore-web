Contact BotAppStore.com

Support for licensing, checkout, license recovery and refunds is handled by email. The address is revealed on request to keep it away from spam crawlers.

Support email

Press the button to show the support email address, then write to us with your question. For license questions please include the SKU you bought; never send a full license code or any private key.

What to contact us about

  • Licensing and checkout: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or SOL payments that did not return a license code.
  • Refunds: refunds are available within 15 days of purchase. When a refund is issued, the license is revoked. Details are on Privacy & Refunds.
  • Product questions: each bot has a page with its inputs, outputs, limits and FAQ; start from the Agent Catalog.
