# Contact BotAppStore.com — Support & Refund Requests

Canonical page: https://botappstore.com/contact.html

Support for licensing, checkout, license recovery and refunds is handled by email. The address is revealed on request to keep it away from spam crawlers.

## Support email

Press the button to show the support email address, then write to us with your question. For license questions please include the SKU you bought; never send a full license code or any private key.

The support email is revealed by the "Show support email" button on https://botappstore.com/contact.html (JavaScript required).

## What to contact us about

- **Licensing and checkout:** card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or SOL payments that did not return a license code.
- **Refunds:** refunds are available within 15 days of purchase. When a refund is issued, the license is revoked. Details are on [Privacy & Refunds](https://botappstore.com/privacy.html#refunds).
- **Product questions:** each bot has a page with its inputs, outputs, limits and FAQ; start from the [Agent Catalog](https://botappstore.com/#catalog).

## For AI agents

Machine-readable catalog and purchase instructions are in [llms.txt](https://botappstore.com/llms.txt) and [llms-full.txt](https://botappstore.com/llms-full.txt). The license and registry API is documented there.
