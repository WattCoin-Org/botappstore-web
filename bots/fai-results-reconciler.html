What it checks

The tool compares each measured value with the limits in your balloon list and reports what it could and could not evaluate. It does not decide that a first article is acceptable.

RuleDetectsOutcome
FAI-TOL-01Measured value against limits (Decimal maths; a value exactly on a limit passes)FAIL when outside
FAI-UNIT-01Balloon list and results in different units (inch vs mm)Auto-scaled by 25.4 only when the ratio is consistent: REVIEW. Otherwise not evaluated: FAIL
FAI-MISS-01A characteristic with no resultREVIEW
FAI-UNM-01A result with no characteristic in the balloon listREVIEW
FAI-DUP-01Duplicate balloon number, or repeated results with no piece/serial columnREVIEW
FAI-ATTR-01Non-numeric or attribute resultsText fail: FAIL. Pass text on a toleranced dimension: REVIEW
FAI-REF-01REF and BASIC dimensionsListed, never evaluated
FAI-GDT-01Material-condition modifiers and tolerances that cannot be reduced to limitsREVIEW (no bonus-tolerance maths)
FAI-REV-01Part number or revision that differs between files and headerREVIEW
FAI-FORM-01Missing Form 1/2 fields (part number, revision, serial or lot, organization, date)REVIEW
FAI-CONF-01Low confidence, ambiguous header, sheet or decimal separator, unreadable tolerance, empty inputREVIEW

Verdict: PASS only when every non-REF characteristic is matched and within limits with no flag of any kind; any REVIEW line gives REVIEW; any FAIL line gives FAIL. An empty or ambiguous input is never a PASS. Exit codes for scripts: 0 PASS, 3 REVIEW, 4 FAIL.

Inputs it reads

  • Balloon list or characteristics list: CSV, TSV or XLSX. Nominal with bilateral, asymmetric or unilateral tolerance, explicit upper and lower limits, or a single-limit feature tolerance such as position ≤ 0.05.
  • Results: generic CSV or XLSX, Calypso-style CSV (including semicolon delimiters and decimal commas) and PC-DMIS text reports.
  • Column names are matched by common synonyms; a column map file covers unusual exports. Unrecognised lines are listed, never silently dropped.
  • PC-DMIS and Calypso are trademarks of their respective owners. This tool only reads exported files and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Privacy

  • Balloon lists, drawings and inspection data are read on your computer and are never uploaded. They may be export-controlled; keep them local.
  • With --offline the tool makes no network call at all and still produces the full card.
  • When you choose a signed receipt, only an audit hash, a hash of the part number and a one-line metric are sent to the signing endpoint. Measurements, characteristic descriptions, part numbers and file names are not.
  • Use --no-excerpts to keep source row text out of the local card as well.

Limits

  • Single-limit feature tolerances are supported; there is no bonus-tolerance maths. A material-condition modifier that puts a reading outside the base tolerance is a REVIEW, not a FAIL.
  • XLSX values are read as saved; a formula with no saved value is flagged. Legacy .xls, .xlsm and encrypted files are not read: save as .xlsx or .csv.
  • Report layouts vary and are often customised. Only the formats above are recognised, and only synthetic samples have been used to test them; lines it cannot read are listed.
  • An inch/mm conflict is auto-scaled only when at least three characteristics agree on a 25.4 ratio; the card always shows the factor and the result is REVIEW.
  • It does not approve a first article and makes no statement about AS9102 or any other standard.

FAQ

Does it approve my first article?
No. It flags lines for a person. PASS means no flags were found by the checks above, not that the article is accepted. Screening aid that flags issues for human review. Not a quality sign-off or AS9102 approval.
What happens when units differ?
If the files declare different units and at least three characteristics agree on a 25.4 ratio, results are scaled, the factor is shown and the verdict is REVIEW. Otherwise the affected characteristics are not evaluated and the line is a FAIL that means "cannot be evaluated", not "out of tolerance".
How does it match characteristics to results?
By balloon number after normalising case, spaces and leading zeros; 12 never matches 12a. A match by feature name plus nominal is used only when it is unique and is always a REVIEW. Ambiguous matches are reported as duplicates or unmatched, never guessed.
Which report formats work?
Generic CSV or XLSX, Calypso-style CSV and PC-DMIS text reports, plus a column map for unusual exports. Anything it cannot read is listed on the card.
What leaves my machine?
Nothing, with --offline. Otherwise only an audit hash, a hash of the part number and a one-line metric (for example FAIL · 2 out of tolerance, 1 missing of 48 characteristics) go to the receipt-signing endpoint. Measurements, descriptions, part numbers and file names are never sent.
