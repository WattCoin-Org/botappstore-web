# BotAppStore — AI Agent Templates & Skills for Grok Bot

15 Grok Bot templates: SEC EDGAR & OSINT, prompt-injection & MCP audits, CVE triage, domain hygiene, print farm, robotics, mill cert, RFQ and FAI checks.

Canonical page: https://botappstore.com/

## Bots

- [Web & API Text Extractor](https://botappstore.com/bots/web-text-extractor.html) — Free, `web-extractor`. Free self-onboarding web and JSON endpoint text extractor with 10 free cloud text extractions per UTC day.
- [Bot & Skill Security Auditor](https://botappstore.com/bots/bot-skill-auditor.html) — $29 one-time (USD), `bot-skill-auditor`. Line-cited static security review of third-party bot skills and SKILL.md files.
- [Company & Competitor Deep-Dive Analyst](https://botappstore.com/bots/company-deep-dive.html) — $19 one-time (USD), `company-deep-dive`. Seven-section corporate dossier compiled from SEC EDGAR filings, USPTO patents and RDAP, with a competitor matrix.
- [SEC & Patent Watchdog Digest](https://botappstore.com/bots/sec-patent-watchdog.html) — $29 one-time (USD), `sec-patent-watchdog`. Tracks up to 10 tickers or CIKs across SEC EDGAR forms and USPTO patents with stateful diffing.
- [Dependency Vulnerability Triage Bot](https://botappstore.com/bots/dependency-vuln-triage.html) — $29 one-time (USD), `dependency-vuln-triage`. Lockfile vulnerability triage using OSV.dev advisories enriched with EPSS and CISA KEV.
- [Social Hub Publisher](https://botappstore.com/bots/social-hub-publisher.html) — $29 one-time (USD), `social-hub-publisher`. Publishing queue for X, Discord, Bluesky and Telegram with lint, HMAC approval and dry-run by default.
- [Domain, TLS & Email Auth Infrastructure Hygiene Auditor](https://botappstore.com/bots/domain-infra-hygiene.html) — $19 one-time (USD), `domain-infra-hygiene`. Read-only domain audit: DNSSEC, TLS expiry, SPF, DMARC, DKIM, MTA-STS, security headers, dangling CNAMEs and CT logs.
- [Agent Council — Multi-Model Swarm Debate & Pair-Architect Collaborator](https://botappstore.com/bots/agent-council.html) — $39 one-time (USD), `agent-council`. Multi-model council: 2-6 seats debate, pair-program or review code with a chaired verdict.
- [MCP Server & Tool-Permission Security Auditor](https://botappstore.com/bots/mcp-config-auditor.html) — $19 one-time (USD), `mcp-config-auditor`. Read-only audit of MCP client configs for hardcoded secrets, unpinned packages, typosquats and tool poisoning.
- [3D Print Farm & Slicer Queue Ops](https://botappstore.com/bots/print-farm-ops.html) — $29 one-time (USD), `print-farm-ops`. Offline 3D print farm auditor for slicer projects, G-code safety, filament BOM, drying hygiene and queue plans.
- [Humanoid & Fleet Telemetry Inspector](https://botappstore.com/bots/robotics-telemetry-inspector.html) — $49 one-time (USD), `robotics-telemetry-inspector`. Offline robot telemetry audit: actuator duty cycles against URDF/SDF limits, CAN logs, BMS cell delta, ROS 2 bag topic rates.
- [SEC EDGAR Supply Chain & Counterparty Mapper](https://botappstore.com/bots/sec-supply-chain-mapper.html) — $29 one-time (USD), `sec-supply-chain-mapper`. Maps disclosed suppliers, major customers, concentration risk, year-over-year 10-K changes and 13D/13G stakes from SEC EDGAR.
- [Mill Cert Matcher — Receiving Inspection Cert & PO Cross-Check](https://botappstore.com/bots/mill-cert-matcher.html) — $39 one-time (USD), `mill-cert-matcher`. Cross-checks a mill test report against the PO and drawing callout (alloy, temper, spec, size, chemistry, mechanicals), with page and line per flag.
- [RFQ Flowdown Triage — Quality Clause & Export-Control Screen](https://botappstore.com/bots/rfq-flowdown-triage.html) — $39 one-time (USD), `rfq-flowdown-triage`. Screens an RFQ for quality, DFARS, CUI and export-control flowdowns against your capability profile, with page and line per clause.
- [FAI Results Reconciler — Balloon List vs Inspection Results Screen](https://botappstore.com/bots/fai-results-reconciler.html) — $39 one-time (USD), `fai-results-reconciler`. Screens a first-article balloon list against CMM inspection results: out-of-tolerance, missing, unit and revision flags with file and row.
