# Privacy & Refunds — BotAppStore.com

Canonical page: https://botappstore.com/privacy.html

What data BotAppStore.com handles, how card and SOL payments are processed, and how refunds work.

## Data handling

We don't share your data with third parties.

- **Bots run locally.** Templates and runtimes execute inside your own Grok Bot `/workspace` sandbox or on your own machine. Credentials are stored in a local file with `0600` permissions and are not echoed in chat.
- **License check.** A licensed bot checks its license with the license service at wattcoin.org by sending your license key and the SKU.
- **Per-bot detail.** Each bot page lists exactly what, if anything, leaves your machine; many bots work with `--offline` and send nothing.

## Payments

Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are entered on Stripe's hosted checkout page. We never see card numbers. SOL payments are made on-chain, non-custodially, from your own wallet.

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 15 days of purchase. When a refund is issued, the license is revoked.

To request a refund, contact support through the email shown on the [Contact](https://botappstore.com/contact.html) page, or reveal it here:

The support email is revealed by the "Show support email" button on https://botappstore.com/contact.html (JavaScript required).
